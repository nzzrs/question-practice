# Testing

## Automated

```bash
flutter analyze
flutter test
dart run tool/docs/update_docs.dart --check
```

The suite runs on the Dart VM. Drift uses an in-memory SQLite database; HTTP and browser calls use fakes.

## Manual Android checklist

Use a connected Samsung Android device and AnkiDroid 2.25.3.

1. Fresh install; open **How to use it** from the help icon.
2. Import `assets/samples/sample_practice.json`; confirm two topics, three practices and eight questions.
3. Import `assets/samples/practice_format_example.json`; confirm the preview shows two topics (Cloud and Networking) and three questions. Open **Cloud → Compute** and confirm its second question uses checkboxes: a partial selection fails and the complete set (`Cloud Run` and `Cloud Functions`) passes. Confirm the `sourceId` values in the file are accepted and ignored by a normal import.
4. Start the sample practice `Compute choices`; answer one question correctly, one wrongly and skip one.
5. Confirm feedback stays on the same screen and every alternative expands. Use the top arrows to move forward and back.
6. Submit a wrong answer; confirm there is no mistake-kind control and **What went wrong?** shows empty bullets. Type two bullets with Enter, paste a two-line Logseq outline and confirm it becomes two bullets with no markers, delete one, leave an empty bullet and confirm it disappears on blur. Move to another question and back; confirm the bullets are restored on the attempt and the error card. Check “I was distracted or read too fast”; confirm a new card disappears. Uncheck it; confirm the card returns with the same bullets. Then check “I didn’t know this”; confirm the card disappears, the answer counts as skipped and the review asks for a concept.
7. On a due Past errors card, check “I was distracted or read too fast”; confirm the card stays due and its review shows **You were distracted or misread. This card stays due.** Uncheck it and confirm the warning clears.
8. Close another session and choose **Save for later**; confirm Home lists each paused session.
9. Finish; confirm the score and looked-up count.
10. Open an error review and confirm the feedback and card creator are visible together, not in a sheet. Drag the divider in both directions and confirm both panes keep their own scroll position. Tap **Turn bullet into card** with no AI key: the creator shows the bullet as the front, an empty back and the optional-AI hint. Add two cards manually, one at a time, and confirm both save. Pick each of the five templates and confirm the scaffolds. Then configure AI and tap a bullet: confirm **Drafting…**, then the filled front and back; edit a field before the response and confirm **Use AI draft** appears instead of overwriting. Confirm **Create card with AI** in a note seeds the creator. Start a draft and tap Previous, Skip and the close control in turn; confirm **Discard unsaved cards?** appears each time. Open **Suggest with AI** (when a provider is configured), inspect the contrast and heuristic ideas and their variants, quality badges and reading times, and add the selected variants. Retry with the refresh icon and confirm drafts stay. Complete a concept card with only a term, then confirm a provider-less term saves as **Definition pending**, and complete a basic card.
11. Open Cards; cycle the cognitive filters Facts, Heuristics, Contrasts and Concepts, open an error row, confirm its preview is titled **Error preview**, use **Create cards**, and confirm the generated cards appear under their cognitive filter. Confirm error rows never count toward export.
12. Complete one error card by adding a card with **Create cards**, then try **Review all** at the top of the error section and **Review selected**.
13. Preview, deselect and delete a card.
14. Set a topic's deck with **Edit topic → AnkiDroid deck** from the live list (or type a new deck), export, and verify cards land in that deck while a blank topic uses the default. Confirm **Edit practice** has no deck row and that decks are topic-level only. Use **Browse decks** in Settings for the default deck.
15. Export to AnkiDroid; verify the deck, Cloze model for concepts, the `Basic` note type with `Front` and `Back` for facts/heuristics/contrasts, and `question-practice` plus `topic::`/`subtopic::` tags. Confirm no card front or back contains a bracketed topic. Share as files and confirm the header block, three tab-separated fields and `#tags column:3`.
16. Confirm no `Question Practice: Error v2` model exists or is created in AnkiDroid.
17. Use Define with web mode; confirm the Custom Tab opens and the editable definition sheet still appears.
18. Configure OpenAI or Groq, load models, save the key, and test one definition. Confirm it appears in a read-only bubble and is saved without a Save button. Define an acronym such as SRE and confirm the bubble shows the full form under “Stands for”.
19. In an error card review, confirm there are no bias/vaccine text boxes or Check with AI button. Confirm the quality badge shows the estimated reading time and flags a deliberately binary or overlong front, a template mismatch and a missing field, and that none of them block adding or saving.
20. Configure Gemini, load models, and test one definition.
21. Seed an old error (or use a clock override in development), confirm it is hidden until 48 hours and resolves after a correct answer.
22. Try selecting text in a prompt, alternative and explanation; confirm Define and Save as note appear. Tap a saved note, choose Delete note, and confirm it is deleted without a confirmation and stays deleted after leaving and reopening the question. Confirm an http(s) link opens. Open Cards → Review notes, use Create cards to add two cards one at a time, and confirm manual entry works without AI. Set text scale 2.0 and confirm the creator has no overflow.
23. On a fresh install, confirm wallpaper colors are on; toggle them off and back on. Check dark mode.
24. Set the font scale to 2.0; confirm no overflow on home, session, review and cards.
25. Erase all data; confirm topics, cards, settings and key are gone.
26. On Home, use the sort icon for name/recency and both directions; restart the app and confirm the order is kept.
27. Long-press a topic; confirm the menu groups **Edit topic**, **Archive topic** and **Delete topic**, and that **Download topic JSON** and **Update topic JSON** are inside **Edit topic**, not the top-level menu. Confirm Edit topic lists **Name**, **Description**, **Priority**, **Minimum score**, **Target score**, **AnkiDroid deck**, **Download topic JSON** and **Update topic JSON** in that order, with no section headings or helper paragraphs, and that every tile subtitle shows its current value. Save a change in Edit topic. Long-press it again and choose **Archive topic**; confirm it leaves Home and the archive icon shows a count. Open the archived list, confirm its practices, questions, sessions and cards still exist, then unarchive it and confirm it returns with its data intact. Edit it from the archived list once, and delete an archived throwaway topic to confirm the confirmation dialog.
28. Open two topics, choose a different practice sort in each (for example Name decreasing and Recency increasing), leave and reopen them, then restart the app; confirm each topic keeps its own order.
29. Tap the **Shuffle** floating button; confirm the **Start shuffled practice** sheet shows a short summary line, the **Questions** field and **Start practice**, then starts a mixed session. Solve or skip every question, then tap Shuffle again and confirm the no-questions message appears.
30. Long-press a practice and open **Edit practice**; rename it and change its description. Confirm the menu has no per-practice deck and no deck row. Download the practice JSON and inspect it: confirm the topic/practice names, `sourceId` on every question, `subtopic` only where set, `correctIndex` or `correctIndexes`, and no deck field. Change one question, add one, remove one, then update from that file. Confirm the preview counts (new, modified, deleted, unchanged), apply, and verify answers/cards/skipped records for matched questions survive while the removed question's linked records are gone. Confirm the practice name and description are unchanged by the update, and repeat with a file whose topic or practice name does not match to see the rejection message.
31. Export a backup, erase all data, then restore the backup. Confirm active and archived topics, subtopics, done flags, sessions, answers, error history, skipped questions, notes and settings all return in one step. Confirm the AI API key is still the one already stored on the device, not one from the file. Try an empty or malformed backup and confirm the current data is untouched.
32. Set a topic's minimum score (70) and target score (80) in **Edit topic**, then confirm practice pills, readiness, latest/average/best cards and chart dots are red below 70, normal from 70 to 79 and green at 80+. Confirm the statistics projection reads **At this rate, you will average the target score by <date>.**, with a date rather than weeks or months. Open **Edit topic**, download the topic JSON, change a question, add a practice, remove a question, and update from the file; confirm practices absent from the file are untouched.
33. Complete sessions that include two different subtopics and at least one Uncategorized question, then open **Statistics → By subtopic**. Confirm each group shows accuracy and progress, Uncategorized contains the ungrouped question, and the section is hidden in a topic where no question has a subtopic. Long-press a category and confirm the pencil and trash appear; select two and confirm merge replaces the pencil. Rename, merge and delete a category in turn and confirm the list updates, questions survive, and deleting Uncategorized does nothing. Share the statistics JSON and confirm the per-subtopic figures, both learning-rate keys, the regression slope and intercept, and the **Readiness** ring on the topic page.
34. Open a topic with more than 100 answered questions across several completed sessions. Confirm the header ring says **Readiness** and matches the correct share of the last 100 attempts, including wrong, skipped and **I didn't know this** answers. Confirm a shorter topic shows every answer and an untouched topic shows **—**. Confirm no screen says "last N sessions".
35. In **Statistics → Sessions**, long-press one session and confirm the row tints, the app bar shows clear and trash actions, and the **By subtopic** description stays "Accuracy and progress for each question group." without the content moving. Tap a second session and confirm both stay selected. Delete them, confirm the warning names answers, cards, notes and skipped records, then verify only the remaining session stays and the statistics refresh.

## Notes

- Widget tests use `TestHarness` with an in-memory database and an `UncontrolledProviderScope` that is disposed before the test ends. This flushes Drift stream-cancellation timers.
- `pumpAndSettle` is avoided where a page contains a continuous M3E animation; bounded frame pumps are used instead.
- No golden tests: font and engine changes make them noisy. Semantics and layout assertions cover the important UI contracts.
